Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to the questions we get asked most.

Do you do full house rewires?

Yes. Full and partial house rewires, done properly and safely, with everything tested before we call the job done.

Can you fix a DB board that keeps tripping?

Yes — that's one of the most common call-outs we get. We find and fix the actual fault instead of just resetting the breaker and hoping it holds.

Can I get a Certificate of Compliance (COC)?

Yes, a COC can be arranged for wiring work, home sales, renovations or insurance purposes — just mention it when you book the job.

What areas do you cover for home visits?

We cover Limpopo (Polokwane, Mokopane, Steelpoort) and Gauteng (Johannesburg, Pretoria, Ekurhuleni), for now, with travel available for bigger jobs further afield.

Do you still do industrial and mining electrical work?

Yes. House wiring is our focus right now, but we still take on HV/LV, transformer and switchgear work for industrial and mining clients — get in touch to discuss a project.

Will I get a written quote before work starts?

Always. You get a clear scope and a fair, written quote before any work begins — no inflated call-out games.
