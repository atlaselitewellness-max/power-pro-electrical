Services

Hands-on electrical expertise across industrial, utility and mining environments.

HV & LV Equipment Maintenance

Unplanned downtime costs you more than any maintenance contract ever will. We run scheduled and corrective maintenance on your high- and low-voltage equipment so small issues get caught before they become site-wide shutdowns.

Transformer Testing & Refurbishment

A failed transformer can take your whole operation offline for days. We test, refurbish and purify oil on your transformers to catch degradation early — extending equipment life and keeping replacement costs off your books for years longer.

Switchgear Installation & Retrofitting

Old switchgear is a liability waiting to happen. We install, retrofit and commission OCB, VCB and ACB units to modern standards — so your protection actually protects, and your compliance sign-off isn't just a formality.

Cable Jointing & Fault Location

Every hour a cable fault goes unfound is an hour of lost production. We pinpoint HT/LT faults fast and carry out jointing and terminations with the precision that keeps a repair from turning into a repeat call-out.

Relay Testing & Protection

Protection relays that haven't been tested are protection relays you can't trust. We run relay testing and secondary injection on systems up to 132kV, so when something does go wrong, your protection actually trips — not your budget.

DB Board Wiring & Fault Finding

A messy, undocumented DB board makes every future fault take twice as long to fix. We build, wire and troubleshoot distribution boards properly the first time — clean, labelled, and built to make the next call-out faster, not harder.

Infrared Scanning

Most electrical failures give off heat long before they give off smoke. Thermal scanning finds the hot joint or overloaded connection weeks before it becomes an outage — the cheapest insurance your site can buy.

Mining & Industrial Sites

Mining and heavy industrial sites don't forgive electrical mistakes. With real underground and surface experience, we work to the standard those environments demand — not the standard a general electrician assumes is good enough.
